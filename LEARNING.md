# Learnings

Append-only. One entry per window, written after the morning review. Never edit or delete an earlier entry; add a new one that corrects it.

Use this to calibrate the conviction check against reality: when the reel-in said "onto something" and the morning said no, write down which of the five tests lied.

## Template

```
## YYYY-MM-DD <topic>
- window: <minutes> min, energy <low|normal>, ideas captured: <n>, morning moves: <n>
- reel-in verdict: <onto something | maybe | it was the altitude>
- morning confirmed: <what survived daylight>
- which test was right or wrong: <morning | money | wedge | you | source>, one line why
- change for next time: <one line, or "none">
```
